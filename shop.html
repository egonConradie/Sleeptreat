Product enquiry

Sleep Treat is presented as a final-year Food Science project. Online purchasing is not currently available.

What is Sleep Treat?
A crispy carob treat made with soy crisps, seed butter, gluten-free oats and magnesium bisglycinate.
Why carob powder?
Sleep Treat uses carob powder instead of cocoa, which contains caffeine.
How much magnesium does it contain?
Each treat contains 155 mg elemental magnesium and contains magnesium bisglycinate.
Is it caffeine free?
Sleep Treat is naturally caffeine free.
Does it contain gluten-free oats?
Yes. Sleep Treat contains gluten-free oats.

Good to know

Product details

How is it packed?
Seven crispy carob treats are individually wrapped inside each box.
Where was it developed?
Sleep Treat was developed in Stellenbosch over approximately one year as a final-year Food Science project.
